THE CHEZZIES FIELD GUIDE

Online Chess for Kids

CHEZZIES is a free browser-based chess adventure for kids. Choose a hero, solve short challenges and face computer opponents in the Arena.

START ADVENTURE↗

  1. Open the game

    Press Start Adventure. The international game is free in a browser and needs no download.

  2. Choose a hero

    Pick a character and begin on the world map. An account is optional when starting.

  3. Solve a position or play

    Fortresses offer short chess tasks. The Arena has full games against computer opponents.

  4. Keep your progress

    Guest progress stays in this browser. Create a free account to save online and restore your adventure on another device.

How do children start playing?

Select Start Adventure, choose a hero and tell the game how familiar you are with chess. Explore the map or open the piece guide. You do not need an account or a payment to begin.

Who do children play against?

The Arena uses computer-controlled opponents with different difficulty levels. This version does not provide live multiplayer matchmaking or a player chat.

Where is progress saved?

Guest progress stays in the current browser. Register a free CHEZZIES account to save the adventure online and restore it on another device. If you play as a guest, clearing browser data can remove your progress.

Does it work on a phone?

CHEZZIES runs in a browser on phones, tablets and desktop computers. The game adapts its navigation and board to the screen. A larger screen can help when first learning to recognise pieces.
