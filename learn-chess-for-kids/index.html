Learn Chess for Kids

Kids can learn chess one idea at a time through short practice. CHEZZIES introduces piece movement, checkmate, forks, pins and defense in an interactive adventure.

How can kids begin learning chess?

Start by exploring how each piece moves. Then try a small position with a clear goal, such as escaping check or finding an attack. Ask the child to explain what changed after the move.

How does CHEZZIES teach chess?

The piece guide demonstrates movement. Fortress challenges focus on individual tactical ideas, with hints and feedback. Full Arena games let children use those ideas in longer positions.

What should beginners practise first?

Learn legal piece movement and how to keep the king safe. Then explore checkmate, undefended pieces and simple double threats. Revisit an idea in several positions rather than memorising a single move.

How can a parent follow along?

Open the progress view to see recorded challenges, games and skill practice in this browser. Talk about a move together. Progress records describe activity in the game; they do not certify learning outcomes.
