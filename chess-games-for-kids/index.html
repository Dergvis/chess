THE CHEZZIES FIELD GUIDE

A chess game with a world to explore

CHEZZIES is a free chess adventure game for kids. Choose a hero, explore castles and solve short chess challenges in your browser.

START ADVENTURE↗

What makes this a chess adventure?

Your hero travels across a fantasy map. Inside each fortress, chess challenges lead through stages to a final guardian. Completing challenges develops your hero and opens the next part of the journey.

What can children play?

Children can solve tactic challenges, play full games against computer opponents in the Arena, or try their hero’s abilities in the training ground. The adventure brings them back to real chess positions.

What is a good chess game for a beginner?

Look for understandable goals, legal moves and feedback that explains the position. A short challenge can be easier to approach than an entire game. In CHEZZIES, children can explore the piece guide and practise one idea at a time.

Is CHEZZIES free?

Yes. The international version is free to play. There are no subscriptions or paid content limits. Some locations open through gameplay progress.
